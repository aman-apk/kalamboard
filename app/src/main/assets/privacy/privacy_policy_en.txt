Privacy Policy — KalamBoard
Publisher: Aman Labs (أمان) — donation-based privacy apps, not-for-profit.
Last updated: 2026-08-09

■ The one-sentence summary
KalamBoard has no Internet permission at all, so it is technically incapable — not merely unwilling — of sending anything you type, or any data about you, to anyone. Including us.

■ What data do we collect?
Nothing. At all.
• No accounts, no identifiers, no usage analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no trackers.
• The app manifest contains no INTERNET permission and no other network permission. Anyone can verify this by inspecting the package — and an automated build check fails the build if any network permission ever sneaks in.

■ What about what I type and the words it learns?
Everything the keyboard learns (your new words, personal predictions, clipboard history, typing statistics) is stored on your device only, inside the app's private storage:
• It never leaves your device and is explicitly excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
• Incognito mode stops all learning, and you can disable learning or wipe its data anytime in Settings.
• Backups are strictly manual: you export a local file yourself and fully control it.

■ Permissions the app requests
• Vibration: haptic feedback while typing.
• Notifications (optional): local notifications such as clipboard features.
Nothing else. No location, no contacts, no microphone, no external storage.

■ Third parties
There is no third-party component that collects data: no analytics libraries, no ad networks, no cloud services.

■ Children
The app is suitable for all ages; since it collects no data, no children's data is ever processed.

■ Changes to this policy
Any future change will be published here. The core of the policy — zero data collection and zero Internet access — is the reason this app exists and will not change.
